# Pectrium editorial mobile reference study — Mercuryo

Reference supplied by user: mobile screenshot of mercury(o).io. This is a visual-composition study, not a partnership or copied asset.

## Principles adapted
- Dark, quiet mobile masthead; Pectrium wordmark remains Geist Sans and lowercase `pectrium.`.
- Clear high-contrast mobile action **Explore**, leading to an existing Solutions route, paired with a minimal accessible menu.
- Strong editorial visual area followed by concise statement and pill-shaped CTA with separate circular arrow.
- Short, readable copy; compact touch targets (44px minimum); clear mobile stacking.
- The site retains approved Design Family 02 typography and monochrome hierarchy. The editorial visual uses neutral structural fields and is **not** a Pectrium logo or a claim of a real facility.

## Not copied
No Mercuryo logos, photography, crypto-specific content, brand artwork or button colors. No blue glow. No commercial transaction promise. The new abstract visual is a temporary original CSS composition. A photographic replacement needs approved licensing and art direction before deployment.
