# Pectrium — Master Brand and Company Architecture Directive

**Status:** Approved brand decision; implementation staged for review  
**Effective decision:** 2026-10-21  
**Supersedes:** Jeclazon as public-facing parent technology company and master technology brand  
**Repository:** ejepsystems/ejepsystems

## 1. Identity authority
**Pectrium** is the approved public-facing master technology brand and intended independent global technology company identity founded by **Evans Jep**.

**EJEP Systems** retains its underlying legal/corporate role. Legal entity names, registrations, ownership and disclosures must reflect verifiable legal records; brand approval does not itself effect a legal name change, asset transfer, or registration.

**Jeclazon is retired from active branding.** It must not be presented as an active parent company, technology company, product, or master brand. Retain historical references when necessary for provenance, migrations, or audit trails; mark them superseded.

## 2. Brand statement
**Intelligent Systems. Global Possibilities.**

This is positioning, not evidence of operational scale or international market presence.

## 3. Company purpose
Pectrium is intended to architect and engineer intelligent systems, software platforms, and digital infrastructure. Capabilities must be described according to their actual development status.

## 4. Project independence
Quincestone, Emperor Armani, and Clesla retain their existing identities, repositories, domains, and governance. No legal ownership, parent-subsidiary relationship, operational status, or public affiliation may be inferred without evidence and approval. Do not introduce additional projects into public portfolio claims until their builds begin and their relationships are verified.

## 5. Engineering authority
The existing ejepsystems/ejepsystems repository remains the shared engineering foundation. Public company branding is Pectrium; underlying legal and repository naming can remain EJEP Systems. Do not rename the repository, GitHub owner, npm package scopes, environment variables, domains, or deployments automatically. Plan changes with dependency impact and rollback.

Target reusable capabilities may include web, app, admin, optional API, shared UI, configuration, security, infrastructure, deployment and project templates. A target architecture is not proof of implementation.

## 6. Communication and design
Use restrained, technically credible, accessible, mobile-first design; original identity; truthful, evidence-led language; and no invented users, partnerships, products, revenue, offices, or operations. Company website is a corporate headquarters, not an account portal, checkout, or customer dashboard.

## 7. Development-stage labels
Concept; Research; Architecture; Engineering; Validation; Pilot; Public Launch; Operational; Authoritative Documentation. Apply only when supported by evidence.

## 8. Transition control
1. Audit actual code, docs, package identifiers, infrastructure, deployment configuration, SEO metadata, assets and external integrations for Jeclazon.
2. Classify each occurrence as public-facing replacement, technical identifier requiring staged migration, historical record to preserve, or external/legal dependency requiring approval.
3. Stage changes on a feature branch and validate. No unapproved production aliases, domain changes, merges, deployments or legal filings.
4. Update authoritative documentation and public-facing content in a subsequent reviewed implementation phase; preserve historical directives as superseded.
5. Require explicit review of CI, lockfiles, build, lint, typecheck, accessibility, SEO and deployment behavior before promotion.

## 9. Document precedence
This directive supersedes conflicting Jeclazon identity statements in earlier repository documents. Historical documents remain for audit but are not current authority for company naming. Product-specific governance remains intact unless explicitly changed.

## 10. Change discipline
AUDIT → BASELINE → PLAN → IMPLEMENT ON FEATURE BRANCH → VERIFY → REVIEW → APPROVE → MERGE/DEPLOY (separate authorization).

No repository, production, or legal migration is implied by approval of this document alone.
