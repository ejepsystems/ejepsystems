## Construction

Original two-form flow symbol; 100 × 100 master coordinate system. Upper and lower paths each use cubic Bézier geometry. Central optical break: 2 units between top terminal y=49 and lower terminal y=51. Both paths use one solid fill and no strokes, shadows, gradients, circles, letterform P, or enclosure. Preserve path coordinates and orientation; do not rotate, stretch or add effects.

- Primary ink `#0B0B0C` on paper `#FFFFFF` or canvas `#F7F7F5`.
- Reverse white `#FFFFFF` on ink `#0B0B0C`.
- Wordmark exact text: `pectrium.` lowercase including final dot.
- Wordmark typography: **Geist Sans SemiBold / 600**; SVG lockup text stays editable and requires Geist installed to reproduce the intended typography exactly. Do not treat fallback-font rendering as final proof.
- Horizontal lockup: 100-unit mark, 24-unit separation, 68-unit wordmark. Logo symbol and wordmark retain their proportions; no additional trademark symbol.
- Minimum proposed standalone mark: 24px for UI, subject to 16/24/32px optical checks. Header lockup target: mark 24–28px with wordmark 20–22px; social icon 1024px source with generous safe area.
